Decisions 2 and 5

Palette

Cream, charcoal, sky and sun, straight off the flyer. Every core value already exists as a token in src/index.css. What changes is the job each one does.

Decisions 4 and 9

Flyer motifs

Four pieces carry the flyer into the app. Each has one job and a place it never goes.

Segmented stripe

16px on hero screens, 7px across the top of every staff and customer screen. Pattern copied from the flyer: nine segments, two yellow, one navy. Never inside content.

Yellow swash

Choose services
You're booked.

Underline for page and step titles. Highlighter behind one or two words on hero moments. Never behind numbers staff act on, and never as a button.

Taped polaroid

A dog's own photo where it is the hero: Your dogs, booking confirmed, the staff dog profile header. Tilt of 2 to 4 degrees, yellow or blue tape. Never in lists.

Handwriting

See you soon!

Book your groom online

One short line per hero screen, in blue or ink. Never carries information a person needs: no names, dates, prices or instructions.

Mascots, one per bookable service

The walk-in services (nail clip, ear clean, anal glands) are not booked, so their mascots only appear on the "pop in 08:30 to 13:00, Mon to Wed" note. The files are 250px: sharp up to about 120px on screen. A larger hero needs the original artwork.

Decision 3

Poppins and Montserrat stay. The only new font is one heavier Poppins weight (about 8KB). Handwriting uses Caveat, which the app already ships. Barlow Condensed could then be dropped, which saves about 37KB.

Decision 11

Copy voice

The flyer's wit belongs to hero lines. Anything with a consequence stays plain, as the current rules say.

Hero lines get the flyer voice

  • Customer sign-inCome scruffy. Leave gorgeous.
  • Booking confirmedYou're booked.
  • Staff day finishedAll groomed.
  • Inbox clearNobody's waiting.
  • Customer has no dogs yetWho's coming in?

Working copy stays plain

  • Save failedCouldn't save Bella's booking. Check the calendar before trying again.
  • Slot goneSomeone booked 10:00 while you were choosing. Pick another time.
  • ButtonContinue
  • CancellationCancel Max's groom on Monday 23 November?
  • LabelDrop-off
Decision 10

Motion

Three small moves, used together only on hero moments. Calendars, lists and loading states never move. Anyone with reduce motion switched on sees the finished frame.

What changes, and what to watch

Build order

1
Rules and tokens

Rewrite the design-system rules this board contradicts, then add the tokens: statuses, sizes, the stripe, swash, script and heavy weight. No screen changes yet.

2
Customer portal

Stripe, hero titles, mascots in the service picker, polaroids, the booked moment.

3
Staff dashboard

Stripe header, blue active tab, title swash, new status and size colours, sign-in, empty states.

4
Website, iPhone app, emails

Same tokens, each in its own codebase. Nothing shared at code level.

Watch for

  • Staff relearning status coloursThe colours are frozen today for a reason. Tell the team before it ships.
  • Blue selected next to periwinkle ConfirmedCheck on the salon's own screen at low brightness, not just a laptop.
  • Flyer blue fails as text2.8:1 on paper. It stays decoration only; text uses the darker brand blue (5.4:1).
  • Rules to rewrite first"No extra illustration", "status and size colours frozen" and the Caveat note all change.
  • The website goes live with the booking appIts turn carries the same release risk as any change to main.

To change anything, reply with the decision number or the section name. Handwriting is Caveat, chosen on 22 November. Sample names and photos come from the app's offline dataset and the public website.
