## 2. Contradictions and overlaps — resolved

### (a) Report 02-F6 (message the customer after a staff reschedule) vs the ROADMAP `STOP` of 15 August 2026

**What the STOP actually covers.** `docs/research/2026-08-09-reschedule-automation-go-no-go.md` decides **RA-001**: "Should Tranche B be authorised to *automate* live staff rescheduling through one legacy-compatible visit command and one durable customer notification intent?" Answer `STOP`; "the manual-contact procedure is retained". The record's "Confirmed evidence" states as fact that staff reschedule paths "do not send a customer reschedule notification" — i.e. the gap report 02 found is known and deliberately left.

**Is an opt-in, staff-triggered prompt inside that scope?** By the repository's own vocabulary, no. `docs/product/terminology.md:28` defines **Manual contact** as "Staff-owned customer contact outside automatic delivery. Record as operational work; do not pretend it is an automated send." A "Tell the owner?" prompt that staff must choose, preview and press is manual contact made faster, not an automated send, and it creates no visit command, no durable intent and no append-only attempt history (the B2 artefacts the STOP deferred). There is also a post-STOP precedent: `CollectionNoticeModal` (merged 2026-09-19, PR #882 — after the STOP) replaced the automatic ready trigger dropped in `20260526120000` with exactly this shape, and `docs/today-command-centre.md:444` describes it simply as "the staff-reviewed collection notice" with no reference to RA-001.

**But three conditions apply before it is "outside" rather than "around" the STOP:**
1. The go/no-go lists "Exact Meta template wording and approval state" as an *unknown*; `booking_changed_v1` is marked "Active" only in a hand-maintained comment (`whatsappTemplates.js:27`) that report 03 #7 shows drifts from `whatsapp_templates`. Template status must be read from the DB mirror, not the constant.
2. AGENTS.md forbids silently changing "notification semantics"; a new customer-facing message type is a product decision. It needs the owner's explicit yes and a one-line addendum to the decision record ("manual-contact procedure now includes a one-tap send"), not a fresh GO.
3. It must stay opt-in and visible (no `notification_log` row claiming automation, no trigger), or it becomes the thing the STOP refused.

**Resolution:** 02-F6 is **REFRAMED**, not dropped: "make the retained manual-contact procedure one tap, modelled on the collection notice; owner sign-off and a live template-status check first." Report 02's reading that the template is "already-approved" is unverifiable from the repo.

### (b) PWA auto-update / stale tab — reports 02-F18, 06-#28, 07-C10 disagree

**Config (CONFIRMED):** `vite.config.js:32` `registerType: "autoUpdate"`, `:37` `scope: "/staff/"` (staff only; customer portal has no SW), `:51` `cleanupOutdatedCaches`, `:59-60` `skipWaiting` + `clientsClaim`; no `injectRegister`/`periodicSync` options. No `virtual:pwa-register` anywhere in `src/` (grep). `src/index.jsx:11,16` installs `chunkReload.js`, which listens to `vite:preloadError` (`chunkReload.js:75-80`) and reloads once with a 10 s session loop-guard, tagging Sentry.

**What the injected script does (the crux):** vite-plugin-pwa 1.3.0 `generateSimpleSWRegister` (`node_modules/vite-plugin-pwa/dist/chunk-I2Z7IWCN.js:93-105`) emits only `navigator.serviceWorker.register(path, {scope})` on `load`. There is **no `controllerchange` listener and no `location.reload()` anywhere in the plugin's dist** (grep). The automatic reload-on-update behaviour reports 02 and 07 describe exists only in the `virtual:pwa-register` client (workbox-window), which this app does not use. **02-F18's and 07-C10's "autoUpdate reloads the tab when the new worker takes control" is REFUTED.** 06-#28's mechanism is the correct one.

**What actually happens, step by step (staff PWA):**
1. A merge deploys; nothing happens to an open tab. Browsers check for a new SW only on navigation into scope, on `register()` (page load) or roughly daily. A tab left open all morning keeps its old SW and old precache and keeps working — every chunk is precached (`globPatterns` includes `js`).
2. When *some* tab in that browser profile does load (reload, second tab, cold open), the new SW installs, `skipWaiting` activates it, `clientsClaim` claims every open tab in that profile, `cleanupOutdatedCaches` evicts the old chunks.
3. Any already-open tab that next lazy-loads a chunk it has not yet fetched gets Vercel's SPA fallback HTML for the old hash → `vite:preloadError` → `chunkReload` reloads that tab once. Already-loaded routes are unaffected; route-chunk warming reduces exposure.
4. So the trigger for a "mid-task reload" is **"a colleague or a second tab on the same device reloaded"**, not "a merge to main". The loss is bounded to that tab's unsaved React state.

**Customer portal:** no SW; a stale tab's lazy import 404s → same `chunkReload` → one reload. Drafts survive: `useDraftPersistence` is imported by `BookingWizard.tsx`, `CustomerDashboard.jsx`, `JoinThePackOnboarding.jsx` (grep).

**Draft-loss claims:** 06-#28 says `useDraftPersistence` covers the New Booking drawer and `BookingDetailModal` autosaves — **REFUTED for the drawer**: `useDraftPersistence` has no staff importer, and `useBookingSession.ts` touches no storage (grep). 02-F18's "the drawer draft lives only in React state" is **CONFIRMED**. The detail modal's "autosave" is a status label for edits written straight to the DB (`BookingActions.component.test.jsx:25-35`), which is reload-safe by construction — not a draft.

**Recommendation reconciliation:** switching `registerType` to `"prompt"` *alone* shows no prompt (no virtual module) — its real effect would be to stop `skipWaiting`/`clientsClaim`, which are also set explicitly and would need removing. The simplest, lowest-risk change is exactly that: let the new SW *wait* until the staff tabs close (an installed PWA left open all morning then never changes under anyone's hands; the next cold open picks up the deploy), keep `chunkReload` as the safety net, and persist the drawer draft to `sessionStorage`. 07's "deploy outside 08:00–13:30 Mon–Wed" convention is reasonable but becomes unnecessary once the SW waits.

## 4. Cross-cutting themes no single report could see

1. **Two settings stores, one-way backfill, and the gate reads the wrong one.** `20260726144001` introduced `booking_policy_settings` as "authoritative" and the Settings UI moved to it; the October cancellation gate (`20261001160000`) was then written against the legacy `salon_config.settings` JSON. Result: every Portal switch is UI-only, the enforced cancellation values cannot be changed from any screen, the wizard hard-codes "24 hours" (01-F2), the Rules tab shows two deposit forms (03-#17), and `mergeSalonSettings` silently re-asserts legacy defaults on every unrelated save. Reports 01 and 03 each saw half. **Owner/engineering question:** which store is authoritative for the live portal? Until answered, 01-F2, 01-F3, 03-1 and 03-17 cannot be closed cleanly.

2. **"Success before the database/provider answers" is one defect in six places.** Drag (02-F1), reschedule modal (02-F2), calendar status picker (02-F3), Twilio `sent` on accept (05-F1/F2), welcome read-then-write (05-F12), resend copy (03-#9). AGENTS.md states the rule for notifications ("never infer delivery from an attempted provider call"); the UI side has no equivalent sentence. The fix is one contract: `null`/`accepted` means *uncertain*, and uncertain never toasts green or writes `sent`.

3. **The dormant visit substrate taxes every area while deciding nothing.** 28 tables and 11 of 31 `bookings` triggers (04-F7), 2,300 lines of browser decoders with no caller (06-#1), settings that exist only for it (`auto_confirm`, `allow_customer_rescheduling`, `customer_intake_enabled` — 01-F3/03-1), the horizon check that lives only in its inactive branch (01-F4), and status-split stragglers that already went wrong (`recompute_legacy_booking_visit`, `suggest_conversation_closures`). The STOP deferred Tranche B; nothing set a horizon for the substrate itself, so "deployed but inactive" has become the permanent, costly default. One ADR (04's recommendation) is the unblocker for five findings.

4. **Governance is now a measurable cost centre for a two-person salon.** 226k live documentation words, 62 % programme governance; 42 text-pinning "security" tests of which the most-churned file in `src/` since September is a *workflow* test (06-#21); a one-off July workflow kept alive by its own test (06-#16); a required `e2e` check that does nothing on PRs (06-#24); three closure mechanisms, two settings stores, two plan homes, two runbook dirs, two archive roots (07-B4). Several controls now exceed the risk they manage. The pre-write controls (migration gate, target guard, auth manifest, parity harness) are worth every line; the rest needs a budget.

5. **Hand-maintained copies with no generator — the capacity engine's three copies are the solved version of the same problem.** Website prices/names/contact/hours vs app constants vs `salon_config.settings` vs `llms.txt`/listings sheet (07-A1/A2/A3), the "14/day" literal (03-#10), template approval status in a constants file vs `whatsapp_templates` (03-#7), the Deno capacity mirror (05-F14), `database.types.ts` regenerated but never diffed (06-#8). The repo already knows how to govern a copy (one fixture list, three harnesses). Apply the cheap form — generate `llms.txt`, the sitemap and the public price list at build; diff the types; read template status from the DB.

6. **Delivery truth is honoured for Meta and nowhere else.** Twilio WhatsApp/SMS (05-F1/F2), welcome (05-F12), broadcast failures unlabelled and unresendable (03-#8), pg_net responses unread (04-F18), cron failures silent (07-C7), Deno errors in logs only (05-F5). One daily "pipeline health" line through the existing `slack-alerts` ledger is cheaper than any two of the per-path fixes and gives a human the signal.

7. **Delete, archive and erase have no shared policy.** Human delete refused by FK while the dialog promises a cascade; dog delete silently wipes bookings (04-F4); hard-delete of bookings remains a staff power (04-F21); the public privacy page promises 3/6/3-year retention nothing enforces (07-C4); message/audit tables have no retention (04-F16, 03-#20, 05-F11). One `docs/data-protection.md`, archive-first UI and one anonymise RPC close six findings. **Owner decision needed:** the retention periods (or soften the policy text).

8. **Prevention is exemplary; recovery is almost absent.** No confirmed backup (07-C3), no rollback page (07-C2), unpinned deploy CLI that aborts mid-loop (05-F6), no uptime check (07-C7), Sentry without release or maps (06-#31), stale-tab reload that drops the staff booking draft (§2(b)). Every control the repo celebrates stops a bad write; almost nothing helps at 08:20 on a Monday after one has happened. The cheapest five items here (pg_dump job, rollback page, CLI pin, release tag, drawer draft to `sessionStorage`) are each under a day.

**Decisions only the owner can make, which block the findings beneath them:** (i) which settings store is authoritative — blocks M-02; (ii) Supabase plan / backups — M-01; (iii) retention periods and erase vs archive — M-09; (iv) canonical service names and prices — M-10; (v) whether a one-tap reschedule message is wanted — M-15; (vi) horizon for the dormant substrate — M-27; (vii) walk-in cash in the till figure (02-F14, Low); (viii) whether public repo visibility is intended (07-C6).
